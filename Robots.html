Current fleet

2026–2027 robots.

Four specialized platforms working together across offense, defense, mobility, and resource support.

Heavy offense

Hero

The fleet’s heavy offensive platform, built to deliver powerful, deliberate shots while staying mobile in the arena.

Autonomous defense

Sentry

An autonomous defensive platform that observes the battlefield, identifies targets, and protects key areas of the arena.

Agility

Standard 3

A versatile frontline robot designed for fast movement, responsive control, and consistent pressure throughout a match.

Omnidirectional mobility

Standard 4 Omni

An omnidirectional Standard platform developed for precise movement, rapid repositioning, and adaptable frontline play.

Support & recovery

Engineer

The Engineer collects ore and exchanges it for currency that allied robots can use to purchase ammunition or revive instantly.
