Who we are

Engineering is a team sport.

MAC RoboMaster is a multidisciplinary student team at McMaster University. Every season, we design and build a fleet of remote-controlled and autonomous battle robots from the ground up—then take them to compete with universities from around the world.

We are engineers, programmers, designers, operators, and organizers united by a love of ambitious problems and hands-on learning.

Built across disciplines.

Real competition robots demand a tight loop between hardware, software, and strategy.

Computer Vision

The eyes and brain: object detection, target tracking, and intelligent decision-making.

02 / MECHANICAL

Mechanical Design

The skeleton: strong, serviceable mechanisms built for speed, accuracy, and impact.

03 / EMBEDDED

Embedded Systems

The nervous system: real-time controls and reliable communication between components.

04 / ELECTRICAL

Electrical Design

The heartbeat: robust power distribution, sensing, wiring, and energy management.

Heavy offense

Hero

The fleet’s heavy offensive platform, built to deliver powerful, deliberate shots while staying mobile in the arena.

Autonomous defense

Agility

A versatile frontline robot designed for fast movement, responsive control, and consistent pressure throughout a match.

Standard 4 Omni

An omnidirectional Standard platform developed for precise movement, rapid repositioning, and adaptable frontline play.
